## Implemented locally

- Additive operation/revision/outbox schema, service-role-only RPCs, idempotent acceptance, leases, worker fencing, clarification answers/cancellation, atomic publication, stale source and serving checks, and triggers that stop legacy writers from mutating operation-owned meals.
- Authenticated operation/status/answer/cancel/snapshot routes, immediate Quirrel dispatch with durable outbox recovery, and a minute recovery cron. `MEAL_OPERATIONS_ENABLED` remains off by default.
- Meal-level agent with read-only owned history, complete meal group evidence, catalogue aliases/servings, typed schema output, generic plan validation, bounded error-code-only repair, and direct structured portion/move/delete actions. Uploaded photos now enter the same operation as stable owned image IDs; the worker verifies their meal association and storage path, sends short-lived signed URLs as multimodal image parts, and publishes only the stable IDs. No food-name or English keyword routing is used by the new operation path.
- Mobile account-scoped MMKV operation outbox, local-first create/edit/portion/move/delete entry points, clarification and retry UI, versioned Watermelon fields, and targeted revision-coherent refresh without a network-held global lock. `EXPO_PUBLIC_MEAL_OPERATIONS_ENABLED` remains off by default. Previous unrelated mobile modifications were preserved.
- Acceptance now awaits the short queue handoff before the serverless response; the durable outbox recovers a failed enqueue or a dispatched job never claimed by a worker. The app checks active operations at one-second intervals, retries a failed local refresh before calling an operation complete, and preserves structured action type on manual retry.

## 25 September: Flash, food creation and meal sanity

- The resolver had never run on Flash: Gemini rejected the full proposal schema as too complex for constrained decoding ("Request contains an invalid argument" on every call). The provider now receives the schema without array bounds; the Zod parse still enforces them. The final step is forced to answer instead of calling tools.
- Plans must now include `components`: every distinct food mention maps to its items or an explicit omission. The compiler rejects dropped, invented, doubled (same food twice in a dish) or unsupported mentions, and those codes feed the repair attempt.
- `scripts/meal-sanity-eval.ts` (live Flash, fixture catalogue, no writes): 16/16 cases correct across English, Spanish, Japanese, German, French, Portuguese and typos, including dry vs cooked rice/pasta, tuna in oil vs water and oat vs dairy milk. Individual runs took 5.6–25 s over 3–6 steps, far above the plan's tier-1 target. Four first-run failures were OpenRouter upstream rate limits at concurrency 4 and one step-exhaustion bug (fixed above).
- Speed: the resolver now prefetches the meal text's semantic catalogue neighbours (with servings) and recent meals in parallel with photos, and catalogue search returns hydrated foods. Live eval, 16/16 correct both ways: with prefetch most meals finish in 1 step, median ~3.3 s (2.1-4.9 s typical, outliers 12-15 s); search-only median 7.6 s at 2-3 steps. The fixture prefetch contains every food, so the first figure is a best case.
- `get_cosine_results` took ~4 s on production because FoodItem had no vector index. Migration `20260925230000_fast_catalogue_search.sql` adds an HNSW cosine index, rewrites the function (same signature/results) to use it, and adds pg_trgm typo-tolerant catalogue search (verified locally: "tuna cevice", "chiken brest", "olive oyl", accents, aliases, brands). This also speeds up the live legacy matcher and the app's manual food search. **Not yet applied to production.**
- Missing foods: the agent can add a catalogue food from USDA (local BGE index plus FDC), cited OpenRouter web search, or a marked `AgentEstimate`, through a fail-closed Jev duplicate check and the `create_catalogue_food` identity lock. Migrations `20260925205900` and `20260925210000` are **not yet applied to production**.
